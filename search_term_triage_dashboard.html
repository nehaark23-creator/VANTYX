Decision logic

Transparent heuristic baseline for hackathon demo
1. Intent signals
Commercial terms such as buy, price, quote, service, near me, booking, order increase promote evidence.
2. Negative signals
Free, jobs, careers, tutorial, definition, DIY, complaint and unrelated informational intent increase block evidence when they conflict with campaign intent.
3. Performance evidence
Clicks, conversion count, conversion value and cost are considered together. A single conversion from a tiny sample is not automatically promoted.
4. Abstention
Low sample size or conflicting signals produce human review rather than a forced decision.
5. Reason code
Every row receives a one-line, analyst-readable explanation so the decision can be checked before action.
